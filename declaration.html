Please read the declaration carefully before submitting your Household Support application.

Before you submit

Make sure the information you provided is accurate and complete. You can go back and make changes before submitting your application.

Declaration statement

I confirm that the information I have provided in this application is true and complete to the best of my knowledge.

I understand that I must provide correct and complete information. If I knowingly provide false information, leave out important information, or fail to report something that could affect my application, the Department may take action against me.

This may include asking me to repay any money or support that I received incorrectly and temporarily preventing me from receiving future support, in accordance with the Department's rules and policies.

I agree to inform the Department if there are any important changes to my household, including changes to my household members, income, address, or other circumstances that may affect my application.

I give permission for the Department to check and verify the information in my application when necessary. This may include contacting employers, banks, financial institutions, or other government and public assistance agencies.
